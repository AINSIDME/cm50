How it works

Simple by design.
Sophisticated underneath.

CM50 distills the entire crypto market into a single, rules-based index — the same approach that made passive investing dominant in equities.

01

Universe selection

Each month we rank all liquid cryptocurrencies by 30-day average market capitalization and select the top 50 that meet our liquidity and exchange-listing thresholds.

02

Market-cap weighting

Constituents are weighted by free-float market capitalization, with a 20% single-asset cap to prevent overconcentration in any one holding.

03

Monthly rebalancing

On the first business day of each month, CM50 rebalances to reflect the updated universe and weights. Additions and removals are announced three days in advance.

1

Screen for eligibility

Assets must be listed on at least two qualifying exchanges, meet a minimum 30-day average daily volume, and pass a basic regulatory flag check.

2

Rank by market capitalization

Eligible assets are ranked by free-float market cap — circulating supply multiplied by 30-day average price — to determine the top 50.

3

Apply weighting and caps

Each asset's weight equals its share of the total index market cap, capped at 20%. Excess weight is redistributed pro-rata.

4

Publish and rebalance

The updated list and weights are published three business days before rebalancing. The index reconstitutes on the first business day monthly.
